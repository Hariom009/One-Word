How do I put the word on my desktop?

Right-click the desktop and choose Edit Widgets. Search for One Word, then drag "Word of the Day" onto the desktop, in small, medium or large.

Can a widget show a different dictionary?

Right-click the widget, choose Edit, and pick the dictionary. Each widget keeps its own.

What is free and what is Premium?

Everyday English, the widget, search, history and bookmarks are free. The other seven dictionaries unlock together for $9.99, once. That is the US price; the App Store shows yours.

I bought Premium on another Mac.

Open Profile, then Settings, then Premium, and choose Restore Purchase. Sign in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I hide the Hindi line?

Yes. In Settings, under The entry, turn off Hindi meaning. The example sentence can be hidden the same way.

Why do I need to sign in?

Sign-in keeps your account on record for support. The widget works without it. The privacy policy says exactly what is stored.

How do I delete my account?

Email us from your account's address and we will delete it within 30 days.
