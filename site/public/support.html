How do I put the word on my desktop?

Right-click the desktop and choose Edit Widgets. Search for One Word, then drag "Word of the Day" onto the desktop, in small, medium or large.

Can a widget show a different dictionary?

Right-click the widget, choose Edit, and pick the dictionary. Each widget keeps its own.

Is anything paid?

No. All five dictionaries, the widget, search, history and bookmarks are included.

Can I hide the Hindi line?

Yes. In Settings, under The entry, turn off Hindi meaning. The example sentence can be hidden the same way.

What does it need?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It is Mac only.
