One new word a day, on your desktop.

A widget shows a word each morning, with its Hindi meaning, a definition and an example. Nothing to keep up with.

Version 1.0 is out on the Mac App Store. Five dictionaries, the widget, search, history and bookmarks: all of it free, and we are building more. macOS 14 and later.

The word, its meaning, and how it's used.

embodiment

noun

एक परिचित विचार का नया व्यक्तित्व

a new personification of a familiar idea

the embodiment of hope

The Hindi line is there for readers who want it. It can be turned off in Settings.

The shelf

Five dictionaries.

Everyday English, Emotions, Philosophy, Corporate Slang and Idioms: 24,000 entries, every one with a Hindi meaning. Pick one and your word of the day comes from it.

Also in the app.

Search every book at once

Press ⌘K anywhere in the app and search all five dictionaries together.

History

Any past day's word, with a month calendar to step back through.

Bookmarks

Keep the words you want to come back to. They become a shelf of their own.

A reading log

Every word you open in full is recorded, so you can see how far through each shelf you are.

Save a word from any Mac app

Select a word, choose Save to One Word from the Services menu, and it becomes today's word.

Related words

Up to three related words under an entry, so you can keep going.
