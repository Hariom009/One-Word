Privacy Policy

One Word: Daily Vocabulary. Effective 10 October 2026.

The short version

Every word in One Word ships inside the app. Nothing you read, search, bookmark or learn leaves your Mac. We do not store your data in our cloud or anywhere else: it stays on your Mac, local and secure. The app has no account, makes no network requests, and collects nothing. The only reason One Word exists is to grow your vocabulary, one word a day.

What stays on your Mac

Your bookmarks, your reading log, your settings, your profile name and the avatar you pick, words you save from other apps, and which dictionary each widget shows. These live in the app's own storage and are never uploaded. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service.

Feedback

The Feedback button opens a new message in your own Mail app, addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and we only ever see what you write.

Who else handles your data

No one. The app talks to no server, and we have nothing to share or sell.

This website

It sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties. The Endorse button in the top bar adds one to a public counter when you press it; that request carries no name, email or identifier, and your browser only remembers that you already pressed it. The site is served by Firebase Hosting, which keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, page requested, time) for a limited period, as any web host does.

Children

One Word is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
