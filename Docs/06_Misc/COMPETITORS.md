Ratings are from the US App Store unless marked. **(3p)** means the figure comes from a third-party
tracker, not the store, so treat it as approximate.

## Hindi + English

We found **no strong player.** The iOS English–Hindi dictionaries that have a word of the day are
small, with 1–18 ratings each. HinKhoj and Shabdkosh didn't show up in this search, so check their store
pages before calling this a gap for sure.

## What this means for One Word

- **The leaders are iPhone-first and charge a subscription** of about $35–60 a year. On Mac they
  run only as "Designed for iPad, not verified for macOS" builds. LookUp is the only polished Mac-native
  rival, and it's a dictionary first.
- **Price:** Word of the Day by LookUp costs $9.99 once, the same as our Premium unlock. Every other
  rival charges a subscription, so "pay once" sets us apart.
- **Illustrated daily words are standard** among the top apps (Vocabulous, Atlas, LookUp).
- **Common complaints in reviews:** heavy ads on the free tier, review and reminders behind a
  paywall, and widgets that stop updating. Our offline, bundled words and dated pick avoid the last one.
- **Hindi meanings** look like open ground (see above).
