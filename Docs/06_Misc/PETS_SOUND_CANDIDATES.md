## Gaps

**No CC0 candidate:**
- **spit:** no CC0 clip is described as a spit. Row 1 is a hiss burst that may or may not contain one. Row 2 does contain spitting but is CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **reverse sneeze** (proposed): only a CC BY 3.0 video on Wikimedia Commons. Freesound's only hit is a cartoon "Reverse Sneezing sound" (751806). It is almost certainly a reversed human sneeze, so I rejected it.
- **yodel** (proposed): nothing under any licence on Freesound, Commons or Pixabay.

**CC0 exists, but the description doesn't clearly match.** Listen hardest to these:
- **murmur:** no clip is named as a murmur. The picks are a "whirr" and a "mrrr".
- **solicitation purr:** the context matches, but no description confirms the high cry inside the purr.
- **demand bark:** no clip is described as evenly spaced single barks asking for something.
- **yelp:** no clip of a dog yelping when hurt or startled. Searches for "dog yelp pain" and "dog cry pain" found nothing.
- **sigh:** the only CC0 option is an "exhaling" clip with no context. The best real sigh is the CC-BY clip 642326.
- **play pant:** no recording of the play pant (the "dog laugh"). The picks pant after a frisbee and before a walk.
- **grumble:** the clips are tagged grumble but titled growl or groan.

**Other things to know:**
- **Old film recordings.** craigsmith's clips (437971, 437970, 479618, 437938) are digitised film soundtracks. They are CC0, but expect hiss and a thin sound.
- **Licence changes.** qubodup has changed some licences over time. 211607 says "Changed from CC BY to CC0 on 2024-06-11", and the page now shows CC0.
- **Fake or human-voiced clips avoided.** Freesound has some AI-generated or human-imitated cat and dog sounds. Examples: mokasza's "Cat Meow" (the page says it was made with ElevenLabs), and voice-acted clips by Wrenasmir, rickismyname, Imp_sounds and khenshom.
- **Possible film source avoided.** I also skipped 222590 "CAT SCREAM", whose "666moviescreams" tag suggests it was taken from a film.
- **Pixabay.** Searches there for basenji, reverse sneeze and dog yodel found nothing relevant. Many Pixabay cat and dog clips are copies of Freesound sounds ("freesound_community") anyway.

## Verdict on proposed new entries

- **chirp:** keep, but only if the listen confirms it sounds clearly different from trill. There are two CC0 clips (842520, 499362), but the two sounds are close.
- **caterwaul:** keep. Several CC0 recordings are explicitly of cats in heat (429191, 827534; backups 172006, 410744).
- **moan:** keep, if one clip cleans up. Both CC0 picks (801280, 632414) are named as moans but are mixed with purring outdoors. If neither isolates cleanly, merge it into yowl.
- **guarding growl:** keep. In 404920 the mic sits on the food bowl of a dog that growls when you come near.
- **play sneeze:** keep. In 678970 the uploader says her terrier sneezes when asked to bark, which is the excited "play" sneeze.
- **reverse sneeze:** drop. There is no CC0 audio, only a CC BY 3.0 video.
- **yodel:** drop. There is no basenji recording under any licence on Freesound, Commons or Pixabay.
